## THE PROCESS (Replicated for every client)

STEP 1 — INTAKE: Client uploads their content to a secure portal
STEP 2 — INVENTORY: We scan and catalog all existing IP
STEP 3 — READ: Agents read the actual content (never summarize, never invent)
STEP 4 — NARRATE: ElevenLabs professional voice narration
STEP 5 — VISUALIZE: WaveSpeed AI scene generation matching actual narrative
STEP 6 — ASSEMBLE: ffmpeg video production
STEP 7 — PACKAGE: Gumroad/Shopify product listing, podcast RSS, landing page
STEP 8 — DISTRIBUTE: Submit to all platforms automatically
STEP 9 — MARKET: Social army deployed (Bluesky, IG, TikTok, YouTube)
STEP 10 — REPORT: Revenue dashboard in Notion, weekly email to client

**Total human time per client: < 30 minutes (review and approval only)**
**Total Penelope time: 2-4 hours automated**
