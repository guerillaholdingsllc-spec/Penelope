Family first, always. And keeping our homes safe is a cornerstone of that. At GAFC, we talk real talk about gun ownership – and part of that is making sure our firearms are stored responsibly.

It's not about being 'anti-gun,' it's about being pro-safety, pro-community, and pro-peace of mind. An unsecured firearm can lead to preventable accidents, theft, or misuse. Our kids, our elders, our neighbors – they all deserve a safe environment.

So, let's lock it up!
✅ **Unloaded:** Always.
✅ **Locked:** Use a safe, lock box, or trigger lock. Find what works for you – from quick-access biometric safes to sturdy gun cabinets.
✅ **Separate:** Store ammo in a different, locked location.

This isn't just a rule; it's a commitment to your loved ones and your community. It's about empowering ourselves to prevent tragedies and owning our responsibility as gun owners. Let's set the standard for responsible firearm storage, together.
