Consult a qualified cybersecurity professional before making security decisions. ## What Is the Quantum Threat? Imagine your business data – things like your customer lists, financial records, and private communications – is like cash stored in a very secure safe. Today's digital locks that protect this data are incredibly strong. A regular computer tries to guess the combination to your digital safe one number at a time. It would literally take millions of years for even the fastest current computer to crack these complex digital locks. Your data is safe for now because these locks are so incredibly hard to break, making them practically uncrackable with today's technology. A completely new computer is coming soon. They can't open it now, but they know that in five years, new tools will exist that can easily crack any safe. So, they store your safe in a warehouse, patiently waiting. This is the "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" threat. Today, your sensitive business data is protected by strong encryption. But future quantum computers will be able to break this encryption easily. Bad actors know this. They are already "harvesting" vast amounts of encrypted data – your customer lists, financial records, trade secrets – and storing it. They plan to decrypt it later, once powerful quantum computers are available. This means data you encrypt today could be exposed years from now. ## Your 10-Point Business Readiness Checklist
