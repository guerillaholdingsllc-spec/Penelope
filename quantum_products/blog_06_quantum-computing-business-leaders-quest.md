### The Quantum Future: More Than Science Fiction

Imagine computers so powerful they can solve problems current machines can't touch. That's the promise of quantum computing. While it offers incredible advancements, it also poses a threat. Specifically, it threatens our current digital security foundations. Our daily online activities, from banking to sending emails, rely on encryption. This encryption keeps our data safe.

However, future quantum computers will be able to break this encryption easily. Experts predict a realistic quantum threat to encryption by 2030-2035. This isn't a surprise attack. Governments and large organizations are already preparing.

#### What Does "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" Mean for Your Business?

The phrase "harvest now, decrypt later" is vital to understand. Malicious actors are already collecting encrypted data. They store it, waiting for quantum computers to become powerful enough. Once that happens, they will decrypt everything. This means your client records and other sensitive information, secured today, could be exposed tomorrow.

This threat impacts long-term client data safety. It could expose Non-Public Personal Information (NPPI) years down the line. NPPI includes any information that is not publicly available. This could be Social Security numbers, health records, or financial details. Protecting this data is a fiduciary duty.

### 5 Essential Questions for Business Leaders Today

Ignoring the quantum future is not an option. Proactive planning is crucial. Here are five questions every business leader should ask. These questions will help you assess your current posture and plan for quantum readiness.

#### 1. Are We Aware of Our Critical Data's Lifespan?

Consider the value and lifespan of your data. How long does your business need to protect specific client records? Does this data contain NPPI that could be valuable in 10 or 15 years? If so, it falls within the 2030-2035 quantum threat window. Long-term contracts, medical histories, or proprietary designs need immediate attention. You must secure this data against future decryption.

#### 2. What Information Do We Hold That Needs Quantum-Safe Protection?

Start by inventorying your most sensitive information. This includes all client data, financial records, and intellectual property. Any data that, if exposed, would cause significant harm needs quantum-safe measures. This is crucial for maintaining client trust and ensuring client data safety. Beyond internal data, think about data shared with partners. Your responsibility extends to protecting all sensitive information under your care.

#### 3. How Does Our Supply Chain Handle Sensitive Information?

Your business doesn't operate in a vacuum. You rely on vendors, partners, and service providers. Each of these entities handles some of your data. Their quantum readiness affects your own. Ask your vendors about their post-quantum cryptography plans. Understand how they secure your shared NPPI. A weak link in the supply chain can jeopardize your entire client data safety strategy. This is a critical area for due diligence.

#### 4. What Is Our Current Encryption Posture?

You don't need to be a cryptography expert. But you do need to understand your current security foundations. Are your systems using modern, robust encryption standards? The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is leading the charge in post-quantum cryptography. NIST published its first quantum-safe standards, FIPS 203, 204, and 205, in August 2024. These standards are the future. Knowing your current status helps you plan for future upgrades.

#### 5. What Steps Can We Take Today for Quantum Readiness?

Preparation doesn't have to wait for quantum computers to be fully operational. Early steps are already being taken. Google has been testing quantum-safe Chrome since 2023. This shows that transitioning to new standards is possible now. Start with an audit of your data and systems. Create a roadmap for upgrading your encryption. Educate your team about these emerging threats. Small, consistent steps today build a strong foundation for the future.
