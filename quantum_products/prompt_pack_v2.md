## Quantum Risk Assessment

Here are 5 high-quality prompt templates for business professionals on assessing quantum computing business risk:

---

*Use case: To quickly identify high-level strategic risks and opportunities related to quantum computing for an organization.*
```

For each identified risk or opportunity, briefly explain its potential impact on our [Company Size, e.g., large enterprise, mid-sized firm] and suggest an initial strategic question or action point for our leadership team to explore further. Focus on business implications, not technical details.
```

## Executive Communication

**PROMPT 1: Quantum Risk Explained: The Digital Locksmith Analogy**
*Use case: To provide a simple, relatable explanation of quantum risk using a business analogy for non-technical leadership.*
```
You are a strategic communications advisor. Explain quantum risk to our executive board, who are non-technical. Use a clear, business-focused analogy, avoiding any physics or math.

Focus on the following:
1.  **The Analogy:** Compare our current digital security (encryption) to a very strong, complex lock system protecting our most valuable assets ([TYPE OF DATA/ASSETS, e.g., customer data, IP, financial records]).
2.  **The Threat:** Introduce quantum computing as a new, unprecedented "master key" that could potentially pick *all* existing digital locks with ease, rendering current security obsolete.
3.  **Business Impact:** Highlight the potential consequences for our organization ([ORGANIZATION NAME]) if these locks are broken, such as [SPECIFIC BUSINESS IMPACT 1, e.g., massive data breaches], [SPECIFIC BUSINESS IMPACT 2, e.g., loss of competitive advantage], and [SPECIFIC BUSINESS IMPACT 3, e.g., regulatory fines].
4.  **Call to Action (Awareness):** Emphasize that this is a future, but inevitable, challenge requiring strategic awareness and planning now.
```
