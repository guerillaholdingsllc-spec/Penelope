## 6 Terms Your Board Will Use (plain English definitions)
Understanding these terms helps you lead. It allows for informed discussions.
1.  **Quantum Computer:** A new, powerful type of computer. It works in a unique way.
2.  **Encryption:** Digital locks. These protect your data online.
3.  **Post-Quantum Cryptography:** New, stronger digital locks. They resist attacks from quantum computers.
4.  **Harvest Now, Decrypt Later:** Stealing encrypted data today. The goal is to unlock it later.
5.  **NIST:** A US government agency. It sets important technology standards.
6.  **Migration:** The process of moving. For example, switching to new security systems.
