## TOP 3 FAILURE MODES

1.  **Giving Up Too Early (Lack of Patience):**
    *   **What kills it:** Organic growth, especially from a zero base, is glacially slow. Most people quit before Gumroad Discover kicks in, before SEO articles index, or before social momentum builds. The first 60 days will feel like a silent void.
    *   **How to avoid:** Set realistic expectations. Understand this is a 6-12 month grind for significant results. Focus on *process metrics* (posts published, Reddit comments, emails captured) rather than *outcome metrics* (sales) for the first 90 days. Celebrate tiny wins.
2.  **Poor Content Quality & Lack of Value:**
    *   **What kills it:** If your blog posts are shallow, your Reddit comments aren't insightful, or your social content is boring, no one will pay attention, let alone pay for your guides. Your niche is complex and demands high-quality, authoritative content.
    *   **How to avoid:** Invest significant time in research and writing for every piece of content. Aim to be the *best free resource* on a given micro-topic. Provide genuinely useful insights. Ensure your guides deliver on the promise of your free content.
3.  **Spamming/Hard Selling:**
    *   **What kills it:** Directly pitching your products on Reddit will get you banned. Constant sales messages on social media with no followers will be ignored. Your audience needs to trust you and perceive you as an authority first.
    *   **How to avoid:** Adhere to the 90/10 rule: 90% value, education, entertainment; 10% soft, relevant promotion (e.g., linking to lead magnet or blog, not directly to paid products, except on Gumroad itself). Build brand equity before attempting to extract monetary value. Let the value speak for itself and gently guide people towards solutions.
